Navigation Guide
  • Use the sidebar on the left to switch between modules.
  • Click a module name to load its content in the main viewport.
  • Use the collapse button (☰) at the top of the sidebar to expand/collapse it.
  • Some sections are grouped into folders — click the folder name to expand its contents.
  • Your current role is shown at the top of the sidebar.
Session & Security
  • Session timeout: Your session expires after a period of inactivity. You will be redirected to the login page.
  • MFA: Multi-factor authentication is required for all logins. Keep your authenticator app accessible.
  • Logout: Always use the logout button when done. Do not simply close the browser tab.
  • Audit trail: Every action you take is recorded. Always verify entries before submitting.
Getting Help

If you encounter issues or have questions:

  • Contact the system administrator via the board's official communication channel.
  • Report bugs or request features through the board's IT support team.
  • Check this help panel for updated guides — content is reviewed regularly.

Treasurer Guides
Add Members
  1. Go to Members in the sidebar
  2. Click Add Member and fill in their details
  3. A Membership Fee record is auto-created
  4. Collect the fee → goes to Auditor → President for approval
Monthly Dues
  1. Go to Monthly Dues → By Salary Deduction
  2. Select month and upload the accounting sheet
  3. Submit batch → Auditor verifies → President approves
  4. Fund inflow recorded automatically upon approval
Aids — Prerequisite
  1. File aid at Aid Requests → Medical Aid or Death Aid Claim
  2. Auditor verifies → President approves → AidTrackingPost created
  3. Go to Record Contribution tab and choose a workflow below
Normal Pay — Flow

Treasurer

  1. Click Pay per member or use checkbox → Pay Selected → sets to RECORDED
  2. Use Skip for members who won't pay | Notify to send reminders
  3. Upload Deduction Sheet (file + batch reference + payroll period) at bottom panel
  4. Record Remittance (salary deduction posts only) — log when deducted amounts are deposited to CAUFA's account
  5. Click Mark as Finished (requires ≥70% RECORDED/PAID + deduction sheet uploaded)

Auditor → President

  1. Auditor batch-verifies → all RECORDED become PAID → fund inflow recorded → President approves

Treasurer

  1. Click Release → post closed → Auditor & President get notified with Acknowledge button
Paid with Funds — Flow

Treasurer

  1. Click Paid with Funds → request sent to Auditor

Auditor → President

  1. Auditor verifies → President approves → post enters pending_release

Treasurer

  1. Click Release → OUTFLOW recorded (fund disburses) → post enters repayment phase

Treasurer

  1. Members pay individually via Pay buttons (sets RECORDED) to replenish the fund

Treasurer

  1. Click Close Repayment → remaining unpaid auto-skipped → sent to Auditor

Auditor → President

  1. Auditor batch-verifies → all RECORDED become PAID → INFLOW recorded → President approves → post closed

Auditor Guides
Auditing Payments
  1. Navigate to Membership Fee Audit or Monthly Dues audit sections.
  2. Review pending entries submitted by the Treasurer.
  3. Verify the proof of payment and cross-check member details.
  4. Approve (Verified) or return for revision (Returned for Revision) with a comment.

All audit actions are recorded in the audit trail.

Verifying Aid Requests
  1. Navigate to Aid Audit sections (Medical Aid / Death Aid).
  2. Review the member's supporting documents and eligibility.
  3. Verify the amounts against the scheme's aid policy.
  4. Approve or return with feedback.
Using the Returns Panel

The Returns & Revision panel shows entries that were returned by the Auditor (you) that have been resubmitted by the Treasurer.

  • Navigate to Returns & Revision in the sidebar.
  • View resubmitted entries and their updated details.
  • Re-verify and approve or return again as needed.

As an Auditor, this panel is read-only — you cannot edit entries directly.

Audit Reports
  • Audit Reports Compiler: Generate comprehensive audit reports for board reviews.
  • Comprehensive Audit Collection: Review the full audit history across all transaction types.
  • Payroll Batch Audit: Verify salary deduction batches before approval.
{% elif officer_role == "president" %}
President Guides
Approving Payments
  1. Navigate to Presidential Payments in the sidebar.
  2. Review entries that have been verified by the Auditor and are pending your approval.
  3. Verify the details and supporting documents.
  4. Approve or reject with a reason.

Your approval is the final step in the payment workflow.

Reviewing Aid Requests
  1. Navigate to Presidential Aid Requests in the sidebar.
  2. Review aid requests that have passed audit verification.
  3. Confirm the disbursement amounts and recipient details.
  4. Approve for final processing.
Payroll & Executive Oversight
  • Approve Payroll Batches: Review and approve payroll deduction batches submitted by the Treasurer and audited by the Auditor.
  • Executive Ledger: View the full financial ledger for high-level oversight.
  • Reports Compiler: Generate executive summaries and board-ready reports.
Board Resolutions & Bylaws

Access and manage board resolutions and bylaws documents:

  • Upload new bylaws files for board record-keeping.
  • View and download existing bylaws documents.
  • Delete outdated documents (with appropriate authorization).
