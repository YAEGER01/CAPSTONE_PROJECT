About CAUFA

Identity, purpose, and structure

Mission

To promote unity, professional growth, and meaningful faculty participation through responsible service and coordinated activities.

Vision

To be a trusted faculty organization that fosters excellence, integrity, and active engagement within the university community.

History

CAUFA serves the faculty community of Isabela State University Cauayan and supports communication, collaboration, and organized representation.

Objectives

  • Strengthen faculty unity and coordination.
  • Support approved activities and institutional goals.
  • Provide a clear channel for verified information.
  • Encourage participation and professional development.
